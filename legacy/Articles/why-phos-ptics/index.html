Why Phos Optics?

By Nikolas Pateras
May 13, 2026
Vision & Care

One of the questions I am often asked in the store is: “Why Phos Optics?” The answer may not be as simple as it seems, but it is certainly worth sharing.

Light is not merely a physical phenomenon. As an optician, I witness every day how its proper management can profoundly transform a person’s quality of life. At Phos Optics, we do not simply “sell glasses.” We care for what is most precious: the very medium through which you connect with everything you love.

Through this article, I invite you to discover what happens when scientific precision meets personal dedication.

The Story of Light

The history of light is, in essence, the history of vision itself. From the moment of the Big Bang, when photons flooded the chaos, light began its eternal journey. Since then, it has never ceased to serve as our sole witness to the existence of the world.

Light has deeply influenced human thought and, consequently, every civilization—from the Far East and the Arab world to Ancient Greece, Rome, and, of course, the Christian tradition. At the same time, both the arts and the sciences have continually sought to master it. We encounter it in poetry, painting, music, sculpture, theatre, and photography, culminating in the seventh art—cinema—where we experience its ultimate celebration.

Countless scientists have devoted their lives to understanding its nature. Galileo Galilei, Leonardo da Vinci, Isaac Newton, Johannes Kepler, James Clerk Maxwell, Albert Einstein, and many others have largely shaped the way we understand light today. We now recognize its dual nature—both wave and particle—while the quantum revolution has revealed that light holds mysteries that often go beyond common sense.

This understanding has led humanity to tremendous advances in medicine, astronomy, communications, energy, and our everyday lives. After all, you are most likely reading this very article right now thanks to the light emitted by a screen.

Why the Name Phos Optics?

In today’s fast-paced world of increasing visual demands, the choice of the name “Phos Optics” was not a random commercial decision. It was made with deep respect for light itself. For me, this respect is not expressed merely through words, but demonstrated through the precision of my work. It reflects the commitment of a healthcare professional to care for your vision with the seriousness it deserves, always offering the best, personalized solutions.

“Phos” is the word that represents the beginning of everything—truth, knowledge, and all that I wish to offer you.

It embodies the clarity and care that I want to define every experience you have in our store. Because for me, light is not simply an object of study; it is a responsibility. And Phos Optics is my way of carrying that responsibility to every person who places their trust in me—to each eye, individually.

A Personal Responsibility

This sense of duty is the invaluable legacy of my father, Michalis. Although he is no longer with us, he taught me that integrity in one’s work is the only true foundation upon which you can look others in the eye. At the same time, it is the promise I make every day to my wife and children—my own personal light—who constantly remind me why it is worth striving for excellence.

At Phos Optics, the people who stand beside me combine scientific expertise with refined aesthetics. They are here to guide you toward the right choices, so that your vision is not only crystal clear, but also a true reflection of your personality.

Because at Phos Optics, light meets science, history, and above all, the human element. We look forward to welcoming you and discovering it together.

And because eye care is a journey of knowledge that never truly ends, through this page I will be regularly sharing articles and advice. My goal is to help you better understand topics related to your eyes, new technologies, and everything that can make your daily life brighter and clearer.

I would be truly delighted to have you join me on this journey.

Nikolas Pateras
